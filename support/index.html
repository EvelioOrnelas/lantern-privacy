Velada Support

For Velada on iPhone and Apple TV

What Velada is

Velada is a media player for iPhone and Apple TV. It plays videos from your own media servers (such as Jellyfin, Emby or Plex) and the live TV sources you add, and can keep your watch history in step with Trakt or Simkl. Velada doesn't provide, host or sell any content.

Contact

Questions, problems or requests: open an issue at github.com/EvelioOrnelas/velada-privacy.

If something won't play, the Playback Log helps: on iPhone it's in Settings → General → Playback Log, on Apple TV in Settings → Video Player → Playback Log.

Erasing your data

On iPhone: Settings → General → Erase All Content & Settings. On Apple TV: Settings → About → Erase All Content & Settings. Either one removes everything Velada stored on that device and in your iCloud, and signs you out of every server and service.

Privacy

Velada has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
