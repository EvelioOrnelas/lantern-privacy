Velada Privacy Policy

For Velada (formerly Lantern) on iPhone and Apple TV · Effective November 26, 2026 (updated: new app name; what's collected and sent hasn't changed)

No account, no tracking

Velada doesn't have accounts of its own. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't collect information about you. The developer doesn't receive any of your data.

What stays on your device

Your settings, profiles, saved titles, watch progress and downloads stay on your iPhone or Apple TV. If iCloud is on, some settings are kept in your own iCloud account so your other devices match. Passwords and sign-in tokens for your media servers, Trakt and Simkl are kept in the device's Keychain.

Your media servers and live TV

Velada connects directly to the media servers (such as Jellyfin, Emby or Plex) and live TV sources you add. What you watch there is between you and those servers; Velada doesn't send it anywhere else, except to a tracking service you choose to sign in to.

Services Velada asks for details

To show titles, artwork and ratings, Velada sends title IDs and your searches to TMDB. MDBList is contacted only for lists you add to Home and, if you add your own MDBList key, for titles' IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes scores.

If you sign in to Trakt or Simkl, what you watch and add to your lists is sent to that service, because that's what you signed in for.

When your media server has no intro or credits markers for an episode, Velada asks IntroDB for skip times. It sends only the show's IMDb ID and the season and episode number. You can turn this off in Settings (Online Skip Times).

Trailers come from YouTube: their thumbnails load from YouTube, and playing one opens YouTube (in a Safari window on iPhone, in the YouTube app on Apple TV).

Signing in to Plex happens on plex.tv; after that Velada talks to your Plex servers directly.

Velada doesn't sell or share your data. When you use one of these services, Velada sends your request straight to that service, and it's handled under that service's own privacy policy.

Do Not Track

Velada doesn't track you across apps or websites, so it works the same whether or not Do Not Track is turned on.

Removing your data

Sign out of a service in Settings to stop sending to it.

Erase All Content & Settings removes everything Velada stored on that device and in your iCloud: on iPhone it's in Settings → General, on Apple TV in Settings → About. Deleting the app removes what's on that device; settings kept in your iCloud stay until you use Erase All.

Children

Velada isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/EvelioOrnelas/velada-privacy. More help: Velada Support.

Velada plays media from your own servers and live TV sources. It doesn't provide, host or sell any content.
