Lantern Privacy Policy

For Lantern on iPhone and Apple TV · Effective November 21, 2026

No account, no tracking

Lantern doesn't have accounts of its own. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't collect information about you. The developer doesn't receive any of your data.

What stays on your device

Your settings, profiles, saved titles, watch progress and downloads stay on your iPhone or Apple TV. If iCloud is on, some settings are kept in your own iCloud account so your other devices match. Passwords and sign-in tokens for your media servers, Trakt and Simkl are kept in the device's Keychain.

Your media servers and live TV

Lantern connects directly to the media servers (such as Jellyfin, Emby or Plex) and live TV sources you add. What you watch there is between you and those servers; Lantern doesn't send it anywhere else, except to a tracking service you choose to sign in to.

Services Lantern asks for details

To show titles, artwork and ratings, Lantern sends title IDs and your searches to TMDB and MDBList. To find subtitles, it sends the title's ID and the language you want to the subtitle service.

If you sign in to Trakt or Simkl, what you watch and add to your lists is sent to that service, because that's what you signed in for. Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Removing your data

Sign out of a service in Settings to stop sending to it. Settings → General → Erase All Content & Settings removes everything Lantern stored, on the device and in iCloud. Deleting the app removes it too.

Children

Lantern isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be asked by opening an issue at github.com/EvelioOrnelas/lantern-privacy.

Lantern plays media from your own servers and live TV sources. It doesn't provide, host or sell any content.
